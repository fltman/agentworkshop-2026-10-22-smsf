# α gen-002: boundary decoder, peer-aware blame

## Mutations (diagnose.js, still pure)
- **blame()**
  - A discovery failure (`NF_DISCOVERY_FAILED`, "no SMSC found in NRF") is checked first and attributed to SMSF→NRF / krilju (fault 3). Before, it was put on holminator.
  - A data error from the SMSC or UDM (detail "answered 400", or cause UNEXPECTED_DATA_VALUE / MISSING_AVP / MANDATORY_IE / INVALID_JSON) is attributed to the SMSF, because the SMSF built the malformed request. This makes fault 2 (RP-DATA forwarded to the SMSC) visible from our boundary once the SMSC decodes MO.
- **moAnswer()** (new): checks the RP message in the UplinkSMS answer. It must be RP-ACK MTI 3 on 200 or RP-ERROR MTI 5 on error, with the RP-MR we sent (krilju CONTRACT). This is the MO twin of fault 1. If the payload is absent, the answer is ok.

  - `opts.sentHex` turns on moAnswer.
  - `opts.expectCause` makes a forced scenario (holminator `?scenario=`) count as expected, not as a fault.
  - If an SMSC/UDM fault comes with a bad RP answer, the RP answer is returned in `diag.also`.
  - The 4-argument callers (index.js, scenario.js) behave as before.
